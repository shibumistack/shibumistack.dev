Storage

Where submissions live.

On the free hosted service, Shibumi Stack stores your account email, form settings, submissions, and private notes in /data/shibumi-forms.sqlite on a persistent volume attached to the server running forms.shibumistack.dev. Your static site keeps only the form endpoint. Deleting a record removes it from the live database; encrypted backups expire within 30 days. When you self-host, the same data stays on your server.

Data handling

Submitted values render as text.

Queries check account ownership. Login tokens are hashed, mutations require CSRF tokens, CSV escapes spreadsheet formulas, and logs omit submitted values and credentials.

Pre-alpha covers contact forms, waitlists, and surveys. It does not include file uploads, teams, webhooks, analytics, or a visual builder.
